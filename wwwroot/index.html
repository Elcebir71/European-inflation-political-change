Interpretation

What the results show

Across the 79 election observations in 28 European countries, the fitted models did not detect statistically significant associations between inflation, migration-pressure measures, and government change.

The estimates have relatively wide confidence intervals. With 42 government-change events, the sample has limited statistical power for detecting modest associations.

This dashboard presents the fitted model output rather than a forecast, causal estimate, or real-world political-risk score.
